Grammar: Past Simple

Affirmative, negative with didn't, and questions with Did you ...?

Quick explanation

Use the past simple for completed actions in the past. Regular verbs add -ed (walk — walked). For questions and negatives use did and the base verb (Did you go? I didn't go.).

Practice: choose the correct past simple

Sentence correction

Grammar: Could and Couldn't

Explanation

Could describes past ability (When I was small, I could climb trees). Couldn't is the negative (I couldn't swim when I was three).

Practice

Reading: Then and Now

Long ago, people used letters, CDs and landline telephones more than today. Families wrote letters to tell news, and children listened to stories on small CD players. Today, people send email, use mobile phones and tablets, and read stories online. Schools now use computers and tablets to help learning. While life was slower in the past, today we can communicate quickly and learn with devices.

Listening

Short script
